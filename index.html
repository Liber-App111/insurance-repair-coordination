QUESTIONS

What this service is — and isn't.

Are you the insurer?

No. We are an independent post-settlement repair coordination service.

Do you determine my settlement?

No. We do not determine insurance entitlement, coverage or settlement amounts.

Do you perform the building work?

The intention is to coordinate appropriately licensed contractors rather than perform the construction work ourselves.
